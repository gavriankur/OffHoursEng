Practice, Not a Project

Multi-Lens AI Review, Before Anything Ships

Adapted from Andrej Karpathy's "LLM Council" concept: distinct reasoning lenses review the same work independently, then cross-evaluate to converge on what actually needs to change.

1

Independent takes

3–4 lenses each critique the same artifact separately — none sees another's take, so they can't converge early.

2

Cross-evaluation

Outputs are anonymized (A/B/C/D) and compared: what each gets right or misses, where they agree (signal) or clash (worth digging into).

3

Chairman synthesis

A final verdict — not a summary. Leads with the strongest point, flags real unresolved tension, ends in one concrete next step.

The Logician

Does the conclusion follow the premises? Hunts hidden assumptions and non-sequiturs.

The Pragmatist

Does this survive contact with reality? Cost, time, what breaks first if acted on.

The Skeptic

Hunts for bias and unsupported claims — cherry-picked evidence, overconfident framing.

The Steelman

Builds the strongest opposing case — stops the others from settling into false consensus.
